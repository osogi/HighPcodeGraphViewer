High P-Code Graph Viewer

The High P-Code Graph Viewer visualizes the High P-Code graph for the current function. The outer graph shows basic-block control flow, and each block contains a data-flow graph of the P-Code operations and varnodes associated with that block.

To open the viewer, place the cursor in a decompiled function, right-click in the Decompiler window, and choose Show High P-Code Graph. Selecting pseudo-C in the Decompiler highlights the corresponding P-Code operations in the graph.
